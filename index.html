About Studio

Architectural Vision, Structural Integrity & Timeless Craft

Founded on the belief that purposeful architecture transforms daily human experience, ARARAT delivers forward-thinking structural and interior solutions. Every space we design is an articulate harmony of natural illumination, thermal efficiency, and refined materiality.

  • Carbon-neutral biophilic masterplans tailored to natural surroundings
  • Precision BIM engineering and artisanal interior detailing
  • Turnkey execution from site conceptualization to final handover
